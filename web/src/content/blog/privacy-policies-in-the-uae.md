The UAE's data protection landscape is distinctive: the federal framework sits alongside separate regimes in certain financial free zones. For businesses drafting a privacy policy, the first question is therefore not *what* to disclose, but *which rules apply*.

## Three frameworks to be aware of

**Onshore UAE.** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the "PDPL") sets out the federal framework for processing personal data.

**Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC).** The DIFC has its own regime under the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, supported by regulations and guidance from the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection.

**Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM).** The ADGM operates under its Data Protection Regulations 2021, overseen by the ADGM Office of Data Protection.

Which regime applies depends on factors such as where a business is established and where processing takes place. Many organisations — particularly groups with entities in more than one jurisdiction — will need to consider several.

## Common building blocks

While the detailed requirements differ, well-drafted privacy policies across these regimes tend to address:

- Who the controller is and how to contact them
- The categories of personal data collected and their sources
- The purposes of processing and the basis relied on
- Who personal data is shared with
- International transfers and the safeguards used
- How long data is retained
- The rights available to individuals and how to exercise them

## International businesses

If you also serve customers in Europe or India, the EU/UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 may apply as well. A single policy can often address multiple regimes, but it should clearly identify where requirements or rights differ.

## Start with the data map

The most common weakness in a privacy policy is not the drafting — it's that the policy does not reflect what the business actually does. Before drafting, map your processing activities: what you collect, why, where it goes and how long you keep it. The policy should follow from that map, not the other way round.
